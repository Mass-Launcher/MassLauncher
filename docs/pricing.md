# What a MassLauncher credit costs

One standard credit covers one Pump.fun launch through MassLauncher. **The number of credits you buy together determines the rate for that purchase.** It is not a lifetime counter and does not depend on how many coins you put in your next order.

| Credits in one purchase | Standard SOL per credit |
| --- | ---: |
| 1–10 | 0.020 |
| 11–25 | 0.019 |
| 26–50 | 0.018 |
| 51–75 | 0.016 |
| 76+ | 0.015 |

For example, **25 × 0.019 = 0.475 SOL**. If you spend five of those credits today, the other twenty stay in your balance; they are not repriced. Buying 100 standard credits together is 1.500 SOL before extras. See the [live calculator](https://masslauncher.fun/#pricing) or [side-by-side quote examples](https://masslauncher.fun/blog/pump-fun-vs-masslauncher-launch-costs).

**Use the quote displayed in Buy Credits before sending funds.** Your account rate, a promotion, or optional buyers can change the total. A MassLauncher credit pays for the hosted service; it is not a fee Pump.fun requires everyone to pay.

## Paying and unused credits

- For SOL, open the payment first and follow its address, amount and time window. If you already sent funds and the balance hasn’t changed, keep the public transaction signature and contact support rather than sending again.
- If your payment screen offers a token option, only the **exact mint address shown there** is supported for that payment. The number of credits is based on confirmed sale proceeds after fees, not the estimate or the ticker.
- Unused credits stay on the account for future launches. They are not a withdrawable SOL balance. A returned credit for a failed launch is a service credit, not a cash refund.
