# MassLauncher Pricing

Pay-per-launch pricing on pump.fun. No subscriptions, no monthly fees.

## Per-launch price

The price-per-launch starts at **0.02 SOL** for your first 10 launches, then slides linearly down to **0.015 SOL** at 100 launches.

| Launch # | Per-launch price (SOL) | Cumulative SOL spent |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0200 | 0.020 |
| 10 | 0.0200 | 0.200 |
| 25 | 0.01925 | 0.470 |
| 50 | 0.0180 | 0.890 |
| 75 | 0.01675 | 1.265 |
| 100 | 0.0150 | 1.625 |
| 200 | 0.0150 | 3.125 |

After launch #100, every additional launch is the floor rate of 0.015 SOL.

## What's included

Every per-launch fee covers:

- ✅ Pre-funded wallet for the launch (rent + bonding-curve seed)
- ✅ Solana RPC fees + priority fee for fast inclusion
- ✅ pump.fun create-coin transaction
- ✅ Metadata upload to pump.fun's IPFS
- ✅ Confirmation polling + pump.fun link returned to you

## How to pay

1. Sign up at [masslauncher.fun](https://masslauncher.fun) or via the Telegram bot
2. Your account gets a personal Solana deposit address
3. Send SOL to it (any wallet, any exchange)
4. Credits land in ~30 seconds — 1 credit = 1 launch

## Refund policy

Failed launches (transaction rejected on chain, pump.fun outage, etc.) are automatically refunded as credits. No support ticket needed.

## Withdrawals

Unused credits convert back to SOL at the current per-launch floor rate. Withdraw to any Solana address — no KYC.
