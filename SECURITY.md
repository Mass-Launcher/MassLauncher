# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

MassLauncher is a closed-source hosted service. If you discover a security
vulnerability in the Service (https://masslauncher.fun) or the Telegram bot
(@MassLauncherBot), please report it privately — do **not** open a public
issue.

**Report channels:**

- **Twitter DM**: [@masslauncher](https://x.com/masslauncher)
- **Telegram**: [@MassLauncherBot](https://t.me/MassLauncherBot) — send `/contact`

We'll respond within 48 hours and work with you on disclosure timing.

## Scope

In scope:

- The web app at `masslauncher.fun`
- The API endpoints under `masslauncher.fun/api/`
- Authentication flows (Google OAuth, email, Telegram link)
- The Telegram bot `@MassLauncherBot`

Out of scope:

- Third-party services (pump.fun, Solana RPC providers, etc.)
- Vulnerabilities in the underlying Solana blockchain
- Social engineering of MassLauncher operators
- Physical attacks

## Bounty

We don't currently run a formal bounty program, but we recognize and credit
serious reports publicly (with your permission) and may provide MassLauncher
credits as a thank-you.
