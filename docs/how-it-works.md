# From a saved profile to a Pump.fun mint

**Buying credits and launching are two different actions.** A deposit adds service credits to your account. Nothing is launched until you submit a launch order from the web app, Telegram or the API.

1. **Prepare a profile.** Enter the name and ticker, upload artwork you can use, and add any links you want. You can also start from an existing mint address. Review the imported details: a new launch never inherits the original token’s address, holders or liquidity. [If the artwork didn't import](https://masslauncher.fun/blog/pump-fun-metadata-image-import).
2. **Review the order.** Choose the profile and the number of separate coins. Check the quote, especially if you’ve selected optional buyers. The website accepts up to 25 coins per order; Telegram accepts up to 100.
3. **Read each result.** MassLauncher submits from its funded launch wallets, in groups of up to five. Five requested coins means five possible mint addresses, not five buys into the same coin. Submission is not confirmation.
4. **Keep the mint links.** A successful row identifies the coin. A pending row is still being checked; its credit is reserved. A definitively failed launch returns the launch credit to your balance, not SOL to your wallet. Check History before repeating an order.

The service’s launch wallet is recorded as the creator, and creator fees from these launches accrue to MassLauncher. If your own wallet must be the creator, read the [terms](https://masslauncher.fun/terms) before depositing.
